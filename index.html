About THE BIG CAUSE

Good company.
Greater purpose.

THE BIG CAUSE is an annual family charity motorcycle and community festival, bringing people together through a shared wish to help.

Discover custom motorcycles, enjoy live entertainment, explore food and local makers, and spend time with family and friends.

Whether you ride, volunteer or simply want to join in, there is a welcome here for families, neighbours and visitors from Sussex, London and across England.

The club behind the cause

A love of the ride.
A reason to help.

THE CAUSE is a Sussex-based motorcycle and volunteer club, bringing together people interested in custom motorcycles, travelling and practical volunteering.

Community is at the heart of the club, with support reaching people, animals and the places we share.

What the club supports

  • Children and adults facing illness or financial difficulty
  • Older and vulnerable people
  • Animal welfare projects
  • Community and environmental projects
