About THE BIG CAUSE

More than a day on two wheels.

THE BIG CAUSE is a family charity motorcycle and community festival organised by THE CAUSE.

Discover custom motorcycles, enjoy live entertainment, explore food and local makers, and spend time with family and friends.

Whether you ride, volunteer or simply want to join in, there is a welcome here for families, neighbours and visitors from Sussex, London and across England.

“ The bike gets us there. The Cause is why we go. ”

A shared day with a practical purpose.

The club behind the day

THE CAUSE

A Sussex-based motorcycle and volunteer club.

Bringing together people interested in custom motorcycles, travelling and practical volunteering.

Our charity mission

Care that goes beyond the ride.

Community is at the heart of THE CAUSE, with support reaching people, animals and the places we share.

  • Children and adults facing illness or financial difficulty
  • Older and vulnerable people
  • Animal welfare projects
  • Community and environmental projects
